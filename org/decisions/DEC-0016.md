**DEC-0016 — Enforced requires interposition.** A target may be labelled
enforced only where the verdict is applied by a component the agent
cannot bypass, with an enforcement-point declaration; an uninterposed
gate is advisory. Accepts RFC 0009, resolving issue #4.
