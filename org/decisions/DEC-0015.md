**DEC-0015 — Clearance governs disclosure, never applicability.**
Verdicts are computed over the complete entry set and are invariant under
clearance; redaction emits constant-shape withheld markers; shadowed
definitions are never silently replaced by stale ancestors. Accepts RFC
0008, resolving issue #3.
