**DEC-0018 — Authority anchors rootward.** Ownership and decision ids
anchor at the bundle nearest the root that publishes them; closer bundles
may only add ownership for unowned ids, and redefinition requires
delegation recorded in the anchoring bundle. Accepts RFC 0011, resolving
issue #2.
