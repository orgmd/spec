**DEC-0014 — Resolution failure is fail-closed and id-scoped.** Errors
are typed, scoped to the offending id, and never yield partial context or
ancestor fallback; gates deny on errored entries; compilers keep the
previous projection. Accepts RFC 0007, resolving issue #8.
