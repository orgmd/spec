**DEC-0013 — Narrowing is structural, never self-certified.** A closer
same-id constraint narrows iff its action set is contained in the
parent's and its effect is no weaker under deny > escalate > allow.
Resolvers must not honour author-supplied narrowing assertions;
`narrows:` is removed. Accepts RFC 0006, resolving issue #7.
