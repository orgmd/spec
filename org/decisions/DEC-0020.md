**DEC-0020 — Staleness has consequences; ownership has a floor.**
`revisit` is MUST for constraints and decisions, and a stale matching
policy never returns allow. Owners should be roles; the root bundle
declares `own.last-resort`, through which orphaned entries are ratified.
Accepts RFC 0013, resolving issue #13.
