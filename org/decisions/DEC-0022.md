**DEC-0022 — Conformance is for implementations; agents get scores.**
Conformance attaches only to deterministic implementations against the
deterministic suite; agent behaviour is reported as a benchmark score
with full disclosure, never a conformance claim. Refines and supersedes
DEC-0003, and clarifies constitution principle 10. Accepts RFC 0015,
resolving issue #15.
