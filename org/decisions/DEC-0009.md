**DEC-0009 — Scopes form a declared lattice.** The default order
restricted ⊑ internal ⊑ public is normative; org-defined labels declare
their position in a `scopes:` lattice in the root org.md. Incomparable
labels do not narrow and are refused; an undeclared label is a resolution
error. Accepts RFC 0002, resolving issue #9.
