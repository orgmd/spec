**DEC-0023 — Revision identity is `(bundle, id, rev)`.** Within a bundle,
`id` names one logical entry and its revision records are uniquely identified
by (`id`, `rev`). Core hashes every revision in numeric revision order and
rejects duplicate pairs. Revision numbers are positive IEEE-754 safe integers.
Accepts RFC 0016 and refines the stale uniqueness and content-ordering clauses
of RFCs 0001, 0007 and 0014.
