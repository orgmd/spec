**DEC-0021 — Integrity is mechanised on a TUF profile.** Core gains a
canonical content identifier (JCS/SHA-256); Extended gains TUF roles,
parent-to-child path delegation, whole-directory hashing, forward-only
recovery, and a bounded freeze horizon degrading to escalate-everything.
Accepts RFC 0014, resolving issue #10.
