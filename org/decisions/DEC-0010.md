**DEC-0010 — Content files have a normative container grammar.**
Multi-entry files follow SPEC §3.1: exactly-three-dash delimiters,
blank-line-preceded openings after the first record, fence tracking, and
LF normalisation before hashing. Accepts RFC 0003, resolving issue #11.
